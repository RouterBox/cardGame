Wormhole Restrictions Set — Locks on the Battlefield Graph

Summary

This file contains 5 named cards, one per race, each placing a Directional Restriction or a Team Restriction (as defined in design/rules.md Section 8.4) onto an existing Wormhole: the Cindral Reach (Materials, a Directional lockdown), the Wrought Assembly (Technology, a Directional lockdown), the Mireth Bloom (Biology, a Team claim), the Panoptic Concord (Intelligence, a Team claim), and the Starweave Communion (Magic, a Team claim). None of these cards creates or references a Unit-type Restriction — Section 8.4 itself states that kind of Restriction has no operative effect until some future card lets a Unit move or deploy between Planets, a design space this file leaves untouched. Every card follows the canonical template of design/rules.md Section 9.1, and each is paid for from the one Fount matching its race, per the mapping design/cards/frontier-set.md already uses.

The Cindral Reach

Bastion Lockdown Line

Cost line: 2 Mass Type line: Materials — Permanent Rules text: Slow. Spent: choose a Wormhole with an endpoint at a Planet you control; place a Directional Restriction (Section 8.4, which defines Restriction) on it, permitting travel only from that Planet toward its other endpoint, never the reverse.

The Cindral Reach welds the door shut behind every convoy it sends out — leaving is easy; arriving uninvited is not.

The Wrought Assembly

Conveyance Directive

Cost line: 2 Circuit Type line: Technology — Permanent Rules text: Slow. This permanent is a Generator attuned to the Circuit: during the Generation Phase, it produces 1 Circuit Point, added to its controller's Circuit resource pool. Spent: choose a Wormhole with an endpoint at a Planet you control; place a Directional Restriction (Section 8.4, which defines Restriction) on it, permitting travel only from that Planet toward its other endpoint, never the reverse.

The Wrought Assembly builds one design, copied without end, and needs the line running only one way — everything it makes flows outward, nothing flows back to interrupt it.

The Mireth Bloom

Rootbound Corridor

Cost line: 2 Bloom Type line: Biology — Permanent Rules text: Slow. Spent, usable at instant speed (any time its controller holds priority): choose a Wormhole with an endpoint at a Planet you control; place a Team Restriction (Section 8.4, which defines Restriction) on it naming yourself, so the opposing challenger's Assaults may not count it as part of a path. Stats/counters line: Combat strength 1. Enters with no counters.

The Mireth Bloom doesn't ask a Wormhole's permission before growing through it — once it takes root there, the path belongs to it alone.

The Panoptic Concord

Vector Interdiction

Cost line: 1 Signal Type line: Intelligence Rules text: Fast. When this resolves, choose a Wormhole with an endpoint at a Planet you control; place a Team Restriction (Section 8.4, which defines Restriction) on it naming yourself, so the opposing challenger's Assaults may not count it as part of a path.

The Panoptic Concord doesn't need to fight for a route once it already knows which route matters — it simply writes its own name on the manifest and every rival Assault stops reading past that line.

The Starweave Communion

Pilgrim's Right of Way

Cost line: 2 Tangle Type line: Magic Rules text: Slow. When this resolves, choose a Wormhole with an endpoint at a Planet you control; place a Team Restriction (Section 8.4, which defines Restriction) on it naming yourself, so the opposing challenger's Assaults may not count it as part of a path.

The Starweave Communion calls this a rite of passage, but the passage only ever answers to the faithful — the Tangle itself is petitioned to forget the other challenger's claim.

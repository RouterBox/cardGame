Wreck Tangle — Game Plan

Galactic civilizations themed TCG.

5 starting races. One primary strength, 2 complementary strengths, and two countering from tech tree.

Tech tree categories: materials, biology, intelligence, technology, magic

One point per turn in choice of category per generator, start with one generator, build a new generator each turn, generators can be hit/destroyed, and built. points are currency/mana for costs on cards

cards of types are like:

magic - instants/sorceries/global enchantments technology - artifacts with instant/sorcery speed abilities intelligence - cost modifications, resources, information discovery biology - creatures materials - weapons/equipments/vehicles/units/artifacts/production facilities (factories)

cards can have multiple types/costs

game is about civilizations in space that all have stuff from all these types, but different strengths/weaknesses by type, think terran/zerg/protoss

Game can be as big as the galaxy and all the stories within it. build your deck, and play

refresh/acquire and trade/main/attack/main/
